Privacy policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

Debrief turns meeting transcripts into a summary, decisions and action items. It is a student project built for the Luckybeard Launchpad 2027 tech challenge. If you have any questions about this policy, email enzo.devittorio03@gmail.com.

What we store

  • Your name and email address. If you sign up with a password, we store only a secure hash of it, never the password itself.
  • If you sign in with Google: your Google account ID, name, email address and the address of your Google profile photo.
  • A profile photo, if you upload one.
  • The transcripts you add, and the summaries, decisions, action items and projects that come from them, including any changes you make.

Only you can see your data. We never show it to other users.

Services that handle your data

  • The website is hosted on Cloudflare, the server on Render and the database on Supabase.
  • Transcripts are sent to OpenRouter, which passes them to an AI model to read and summarise. Please don't add anything you wouldn't want shared with that service.
  • Welcome and password-reset emails are sent through EmailJS.

Google

When you sign in with Google, we receive only your basic profile: your name, email address and profile photo.

Connecting Google Calendar is optional. We use it only to add events that you choose to create from your action items. We never read, change or delete your existing events. The access Google gives us is stored encrypted. You can disconnect at any time on your Account page, or at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Debrief's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

What we don't do

  • No ads.
  • We don't sell your data or share it with anyone beyond the services listed above.
  • No tracking or analytics cookies. We use cookies only to keep you logged in and to complete Google sign-in safely.

Deleting your data

You can delete any meeting, decision or action item at any time. Deleting your account on the Account page permanently removes your account and everything it owns.
